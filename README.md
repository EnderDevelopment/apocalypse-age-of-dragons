# Apocalypse: Age of Dragons

A massive, hardcore, fantasy-themed Minecraft modpack that transforms vanilla Minecraft into a much harder, longer, and more rewarding RPG survival experience. The modpack focuses on exploration, progression, dangerous enemies, epic boss fights, rare resources, huge structures, and an evolving world.

## Install

1. Install the required mod loader (Fabric, Forge, or NeoForge).
2. Download the modpack from the provided link.
3. Place the modpack in the mods folder of your Minecraft installation.
4. Launch Minecraft and enjoy the Apocalypse: Age of Dragons experience.

## Use

1. Explore the Overworld, Nether, and End to discover procedurally generated structures such as abandoned kingdoms, underground megadungeons, giant labyrinths, bandit camps, ancient dwarven mines, floating islands, dragon lairs, and rare mythical structures.
2. Progress through the dungeon system, facing unique mechanics, multiple floors, hidden loot rooms, unique enemies, minibosses, and final bosses.
3. Engage in challenging boss fights with distinct attacks, phases, abilities, and weaknesses.
4. Use the `/dungeon` command to open the dungeon progression menu and the `/boss` command to list all available bosses and their locations.
